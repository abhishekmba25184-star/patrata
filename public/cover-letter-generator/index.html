Questions about the cover letter and hr email writer

Will the AI make things up?

It is told to use only what your resume says. Where a number would help but is missing, it leaves a gap in [brackets] for you to fill in, and any figure that is not in your resume or the job post is replaced with a gap.

Is my name or phone number sent to the AI?

No. Your name, email, phone number and links are removed in your browser first, and put back into the letter on your device.

How does the referral finder work?

It opens LinkedIn and Google searches for people at the company, starting with alumni of your college, and writes a short, polite referral request you can copy.

Can I send the email directly?

Select Open in my email app to start the email with the subject and text filled in. Attach your resume there yourself.
