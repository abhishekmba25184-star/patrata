Privacy notice

This notice explains what Patrata does with your information. Contact: the contact address that will be listed here.

Your resume

The file is read inside your browser. We do not upload or save it, and we run no accounts or resume database. Closing the page clears it.

Job search

When live search is on, our server receives the role type, city and experience level you chose, and passes a search phrase to the job search providers we use (Careerjet and OpenWeb Ninja JSearch). Careerjet also requires your IP address and browser type with each search. Your resume is not sent.

AI features

The AI rewrite, the resume builder's AI buttons, the cover letter and email writer and the mock interview work only if you tick the AI consent box and press a button. Then your resume text, the job description, and for the mock interview your questions and answers, go through our server to Google's Gemini API. Your name and contact details are put back into letters on your own device. The software first removes email addresses, phone numbers, links, your detected name and some personal lines. Automatic removal can miss details, so delete anything private before using it. Depending on the plan used, Google may keep this text, have people review it and use it to improve its services. See Google's Gemini API terms.

Saved on your device

The resume builder keeps your draft, and the mock interview keeps your recent scores, in your browser's storage on this device so you can continue later. They are not sent to our server. Clearing your browser data removes them. The resume you add is also kept in this browser tab until you close it, so other tools on the site can use it without a second upload.

Technical records

Our host (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. For the daily AI limit we store a scrambled code made from your IP address and the date for up to one day.

Your choices

You can use job matching and the resume score without the AI rewrite. To withdraw consent, simply stop using that feature. For questions, requests or complaints, email us. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India where the law provides for it.

Age

This service is for people aged 18 or older.
