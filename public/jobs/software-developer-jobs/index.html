Software developer jobs for freshers and students

Developers build and maintain the applications a company runs on. Entry openings are usually titled software engineer, SDE 1, backend developer or frontend developer.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

Most employers look for B.Tech, B.E., BCA or MCA candidates, though a strong set of working projects can carry other degrees.

What your resume should show

  • Two or three deployed projects with a public code link
  • One language known well, with data structures practice
  • A line on what you tested, measured or made faster

Interview preparation

Expect an online coding round, then one or two interviews on data structures, your projects and basic database and networking concepts.
