Who is eligible

Open to most degrees. MBA Marketing candidates are preferred for brand management, while digital and content roles reward a visible portfolio.

What your resume should show

  • A campaign you ran, with spend, reach and result
  • Writing or creative samples in one linked folder
  • Comfort with Google Analytics or an ad platform

Interview preparation

Expect to critique a campaign and to plan how you would launch a product to a defined audience on a fixed budget.
