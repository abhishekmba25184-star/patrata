Finance jobs for MBA, B.Com and BBA freshers

Finance openings span financial analysis, credit, equity research, treasury, audit and business finance. The daily work and exams that help differ widely between them.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

MBA Finance, B.Com, BBA, CA and CFA candidates are the usual fit. Check each post, because audit roles often ask for CA articleship while analyst roles may not.

What your resume should show

  • A three statement or valuation model you built yourself
  • Internship work described with deal size, portfolio size or time saved
  • Certifications such as NISM or CFA level progress, if you hold them

Interview preparation

Expect accounting linkages, valuation basics, a view on one listed company and a spreadsheet exercise.
