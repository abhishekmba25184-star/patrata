Operations and supply chain jobs for freshers

Operations teams keep goods and services moving: warehousing, procurement, planning, process quality and vendor management.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

Engineering and MBA Operations candidates are common, and many execution roles take any graduate. Check shift timing and plant or field location.

What your resume should show

  • A process you shortened or an error rate you reduced
  • Event or inventory planning with real volumes
  • Lean or Six Sigma training if completed

Interview preparation

Expect situational questions on delays and bottlenecks, plus basic inventory and process measures.
