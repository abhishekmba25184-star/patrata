Business analyst, strategy and consulting jobs

Roles include business analyst, management trainee, founder's office and consulting analyst. The work is structured problem solving for leadership teams.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

MBA candidates and strong undergraduates. Founder's office posts often welcome any degree with clear ownership experience.

What your resume should show

  • Case competition results with the number of teams
  • A problem you sized, analysed and recommended a fix for
  • Clear slides and a working spreadsheet

Interview preparation

Expect case interviews, guesstimates and detailed questions on your own projects.
