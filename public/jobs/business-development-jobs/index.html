Business development and sales jobs for freshers

These roles find customers and keep them: inside sales, relationship management, enterprise sales and partnerships. They are among the most common first jobs after graduation.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

Usually open to any graduate. Read the pay structure carefully and ask how much is fixed and how much depends on targets.

What your resume should show

  • Sponsorship or fundraising amounts you secured
  • Targets you carried and how far you met them
  • Languages you can sell in

Interview preparation

Expect a role play, questions on handling rejection and a short pitch for the company's product.
