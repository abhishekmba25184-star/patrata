Associate product manager jobs and internships

Product managers decide what gets built and why, working between users, engineers and business teams. Entry seats are few and competitive.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

Often filled by engineering or MBA graduates, though some posts ask for one or two years of experience even at associate level.

What your resume should show

  • A product teardown or requirements document
  • User interviews you ran and what changed afterwards
  • Any product, app or process you took from idea to use

Interview preparation

Expect product design, metrics and prioritisation questions.
