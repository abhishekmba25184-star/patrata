Data analyst jobs for freshers and students

Analysts turn raw records into answers a business can act on: why sales dipped, which customers leave, where cost is leaking.

Check which jobs I am eligible for

Who is eligible

Open to engineering, statistics, mathematics, economics, commerce and MBA candidates. Proof of SQL and spreadsheet skill matters more than the degree name.

What your resume should show

  • A dashboard you built, with the question it answers
  • SQL joins and grouping that you can write without help
  • One analysis that ended in a recommendation

Interview preparation

Expect a SQL or Excel test and a short case where you explain what data you would pull and why.
