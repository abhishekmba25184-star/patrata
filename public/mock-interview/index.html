Questions about the ai mock interview

Can I answer by speaking?

Yes, in Chrome and Edge on a computer or Android phone. Select Answer by speaking and allow the microphone; your words appear in the answer box, and you can correct them before submitting.

What kinds of interviews can I practise?

Mixed, HR and behavioural, role and technical, a resume deep-dive, or case and guesstimate questions, at fresher, experienced or internship level.

How is my answer scored?

On structure, relevance to the question, specific evidence and clarity, from 0 to 10. A stronger answer is written only from facts in your resume and your answer.

Are my answers saved?

Only your recent scores are kept in this browser so you can track progress. Answers are not stored on our server.
