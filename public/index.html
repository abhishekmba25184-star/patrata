          Questions students ask

          Is it free for students?

          Yes. Uploading a resume, seeing matched jobs, the eligibility check and the resume score are free. The AI rewrite has a daily limit.

          Do you store my resume?

          No. Your file is read inside your browser. If you ask for an AI rewrite, the text is sent to the AI service after email, phone number, links and name are removed as far as the software can detect them.

          Do you show jobs from LinkedIn and Naukri?

          Matched openings come from company career pages and, where live search is switched on, from Google for Jobs listings that may point to large job sites. We also give you ready-made searches that open on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and others.

          How is eligibility decided?

          The site reads experience, batch year, degree and CGPA requirements from the job text and compares them with your profile. It can miss or misread a condition, so always read the full post before applying.

          Will this guarantee me a job?

          No. It helps you find suitable openings and present your real experience well. Employers make every hiring decision.
