Questions about the resume builder

Is the resume builder free?

Yes. Building, downloading PDF and Word files, and the ATS score are all free. The AI rewrite buttons are free too, within a daily limit.

Are these templates ATS friendly?

Yes. All three use one column, standard headings and real text, which applicant tracking software reads in the right order. They avoid tables, icons and text boxes.

Where is my resume saved?

Only in this browser on this device, so you can come back and continue. It is not uploaded to our server.

How do I save it as a PDF?

Select Download PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the print window. The text stays selectable, which matters for ATS software.
