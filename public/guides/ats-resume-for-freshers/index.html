ATS resume for freshers: a practical format guide

Many employers collect applications in an applicant tracking system, usually shortened to ATS. It stores your resume as text and lets recruiters search and filter it. A resume that loses its text, or never uses the words a recruiter searches for, is easy to miss.

Make the file readable

  • Export a PDF from a word processor. Do not submit a scan or a photo.
  • Use one column. Tables, text boxes and sidebars can scramble the reading order.
  • Write headings as plain words: Education, Experience, Projects, Skills.
  • Keep contact details in the page body, not in the header area.

Write points that show a result

Use this pattern: action, what you did it to, and what changed. "Responsible for social media" says little. "Planned 24 posts for a college festival page and grew followers from 800 to 1,400" gives a recruiter something to ask about. Use only numbers you can explain.

Use the job's own words, honestly

If a post asks for financial modelling and you have built models, say financial modelling. Never paste hidden keywords or list tools you have not used. Interviews expose it quickly.

Order for a fresher

  1. Name and contact details
  2. Two line summary naming your target role
  3. Education with year and CGPA or percentage
  4. Internships and work
  5. Projects
  6. Skills and certifications
  7. Positions of responsibility and achievements
