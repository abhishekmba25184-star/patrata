How to spot fake job offers

Students are frequent targets of recruitment fraud. Pause when you see any of these.

Warning signs

  • Any request for money: registration, training, security deposit, laptop or verification fee.
  • An offer without an interview.
  • Mail from a free email address claiming to be a large company.
  • Pressure to act within hours.
  • Requests for one time passwords, bank details or identity documents before a real offer process.

Check before you share anything

Find the role on the company's own website, typed into your browser by you. If it is not there, contact the company through details listed on that site.

If you have been targeted

In India you can report online fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cyber crime helpline 1930. Contact your bank at once if money has moved.
