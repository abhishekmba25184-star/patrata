Off-campus job search: a weekly plan for students

Applying to everything feels productive and rarely is. A small, steady routine does better.

Choose your target

Write down two role types, three cities and the eligibility you hold: batch, degree, CGPA and months of experience. Skip posts that fail a stated hard requirement.

A week that works

  1. Monday: collect fifteen suitable openings, preferring company career pages.
  2. Tuesday and Wednesday: tailor your resume for the best eight and apply.
  3. Thursday: write to alumni at those companies with a short, specific question.
  4. Friday: practise one test or case.
  5. Saturday: record what replied and adjust.

Track it

A simple sheet with company, role, date, link and status stops duplicate applications and shows which resume version gets replies.
