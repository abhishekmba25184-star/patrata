MBA fresher resume: what to put first and what to cut

An MBA resume has little room and much to say. The goal is not to list everything. It is to make one target role look like the obvious next step.

Pick the role before editing

Keep a separate version for finance, marketing, consulting and operations. Move the most relevant internship, project and competition to the top of each.

Summer internship

Give it the most space. State the business problem, your method, and what the team did with your work. If results are confidential, describe scale with ranges you are permitted to share.

Case competitions and live projects

Name the organiser, your position and the number of participating teams if you know it. A live project is worth including when you can say what you delivered.

Cut these

  • School level activities, unless exceptional
  • Long lists of soft skills
  • Photo, parents' names, date of birth and home address
  • Declarations and signatures, unless an employer asks
